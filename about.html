A Technician You Can Trust

Manoel has spent years working in the HVAC industry in San Antonio, learning the trade from the ground up and building hands-on experience across homes throughout Bexar County. He started Polar Bear Air Conditioning & Heating to offer something simpler: honest recommendations, dependable work, and direct communication from start to finish.

When you call, text, or schedule service, you're talking directly to the person doing the work. That means no dispatchers, no sales pressure, and no guessing about who is showing up at your home. Manoel explains the issue clearly, gives you a straightforward price, and focuses on solving the problem the right way.

Whether it's an urgent repair in the middle of summer, a replacement system, or routine maintenance before the heat sets in, the goal is the same every time: show up, communicate clearly, and leave you with a system you can count on.
