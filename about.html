A Technician You Can Trust

Manuel has spent years working in the HVAC industry in San Antonio, learning the trade from the ground up. After earning his certifications and gaining hands-on experience servicing hundreds of homes across Bexar County, he decided to go out on his own — so he could do things the right way, every time.

When you call Manuel's AC & Cooling, you're talking directly to the person doing the work. No dispatchers, no middlemen. Manuel shows up on time, explains what's wrong in plain language, and gives you an honest price before touching anything.

Whether it's a repair in the middle of July or a new install before summer hits, Manuel treats every job like he's working on his own home.
